# fm1-wheel

A tonewheel organ firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1, after setBfree.

The full setBfree-derived chain (91 tonewheels, scanner, preamp, reverb and
Leslie) runs on the FM-1 and in the browser emulator. The 27 keys play the
organ; `GLO` turns them into drawbar controls while an external MIDI
controller keeps playing.

See `MANUAL.md` (English) and `MANUAL.pt.md` (Português) in the repository for
the controls, the drawbar-key mode and MIDI.
