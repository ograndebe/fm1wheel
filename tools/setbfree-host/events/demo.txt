# fm1-wheel reference script: a chord, a drawbar change, percussion and the
# Leslie ramping to fast. Times are implicit: each event applies at the next
# 128-sample block (2.9 ms at 44.1 kHz).

drawbars U 888000000
swell 127
reverb 40
leslie slow

noteon 1 48 100
noteon 1 52 100
noteon 1 55 100
wait 1500
noteoff 1 48
noteoff 1 52
noteoff 1 55
wait 500

drawbars U 888800000
noteon 1 60 100
noteon 1 64 100
wait 1000

perc on
perc harmonic 2
perc decay fast
noteon 1 67 100
wait 1000
noteoff 1 60
noteoff 1 64
noteoff 1 67
wait 500

leslie fast
noteon 1 60 100
wait 2000
noteoff 1 60
wait 2000
