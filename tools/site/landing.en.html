What fm1-wheel is

It is not a per-voice organ: it is a shared tonewheel generator, like the original instrument. The timbre comes from the wheels, the contacts, the drawbars and the analogue chain.

Tone generator

91 wheels

Mechanical frequencies (gear ratios), 27 buses, terminal taper and crosstalk, wiring and compartments. The wheels are shared between the keys.

setBfree chain

From scanner to Leslie

V1–V3/C1–C3 scanner vibrato/chorus, tube preamp, Schroeder reverb and Leslie with Doppler, reflections and acceleration.

FM-1

27 keys + MIDI

Built-in keyboard, USB-MIDI and TRS-MIDI. Drawbar-key mode turns the keys into drawbar controls while an external controller keeps playing.

Start in three steps

You can listen now in the browser, or install on the FM-1 when the device is connected.

Install

Connect the FM-1 with a USB data cable and use the web installer in Chrome/Edge. The installer can also restore the official firmware.

Play

The 27 keys play the upper manual. An external MIDI controller can play upper, lower and pedal on channels 1, 2 and 3.

Edit

Press GLO: the keys stop playing and push/pull the drawbars. External MIDI keeps sounding.

Drawbar-key mode

With the mode on, each flat / natural pair controls one drawbar of the selected manual. The black key pushes in (decreases); the white key pulls out (increases). The bar fills from top to bottom: 8 is fully pulled out, at maximum volume. All 18 control keys light equally; the others stay dark.

GLO toggles the mode. External MIDI keeps playing — the built-in keyboard becomes the registration control.

F3, C4, F4 and C5 are ignored. D#5, E5, F5, F#5 and G5 are free.
